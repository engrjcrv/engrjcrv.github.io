# Privacy Policy **Last updated: October 11, 2026** ## 1. Overview This personal utility uses rclone to transfer and back up files to a Google Drive account authorized by its operator. ## 2. Google Account Data The utility uses Google's OAuth authorization process to request access to the Google Drive account selected by the account owner. The access available to the utility depends on the permissions granted. ## 3. How Data Is Used Google Drive access is used to list, transfer, and manage files for the operator's backup and recovery tasks. Google account data is not intentionally sold or used for advertising. ## 4. Storage and Security Backup files are stored in the destination selected by the operator. Encryption, credential storage, access controls, and retention depend on the actual configuration and services used. The operator is responsible for protecting authentication credentials and backup recovery information. ## 5. Sharing The utility does not intentionally share Google user data with third parties except as necessary to provide the requested storage and transfer service. ## 6. Retention and Revoking Access Files are retained according to the operator's backup and retention decisions. The account owner can revoke the utility's Google access through their Google Account security settings. ## 7. Contact For questions about this utility, use the support contact email listed on its Google OAuth consent screen.
