Personal Drive Backup Utility

This is a personal utility used by its operator to back up and recover files using rclone and Google Drive.

The operator initiates transfers to a Google Drive account authorized through Google's OAuth process. This utility is intended for personal backup and recovery, not as a public file-sharing service.

This website provides information about the utility and its data practices. It does not itself upload files or collect Google account credentials.
